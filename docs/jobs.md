# Jobs

The Jobs page tracks applications through Applied → Assessment → Screen → Interview → Offer, with rejected, withdrawn and quiet ones (no reply in 30 days) in a collapsed list. The top shows applications this week against your weekly target, the response rate on applications older than two weeks, active screens and interviews, and follow-ups due (two weeks with no reply). If your latest 30 tracked applications produced fewer than two human screens, it says so, per the career kit's rule. Today's rail has a **Job search** card, and Applications tasks get **Log an application** and **Open Jobs** buttons. A job in assessment, screen or interview can add a prep task to your plan.

It fills itself in three ways:

- **Your job Gmail (desktop).** In **Settings → Jobs**, enter the Gmail you apply with and a Google App Password (2-Step Verification on, then [myaccount.google.com/apppasswords](https://myaccount.google.com/apppasswords)). Jarvis opens the mailbox read-only over IMAP every 15 minutes and recognizes LinkedIn "application sent/viewed" emails, confirmations from Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, Workday, SmartRecruiters, iCIMS and similar systems, assessment invites (HackerRank, CodeSignal, HireVue…), interview scheduling, rejections and offers. Job-board digests (Runway, LinkedIn alerts) and newsletters are ignored. **Preview first scan** shows what the last 120 days would add before anything changes. Emails it isn't sure about wait under **Check these emails**: add them, link them to an application, or dismiss them. Updates only ever move a job forward. Only the sender, subject, date and Message-ID are kept; bodies are read on this computer and dropped. The App Password is encrypted with the OS keychain (Electron `safeStorage`) in `config/jobs-inbox.local.json` and never synced; the scan state is `data/jobs-scan.local.json`.
- **The “I applied” bookmark.** Settings → Jobs has a bookmark that opens jarvis.example.com with the page's link and title, so company and role fill in from LinkedIn, Runway, Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby and Workday pages. Drag it to the bookmarks bar on a computer; on the iPhone, copy it and paste it as a Safari bookmark's address. It needs Cloud sync, since it opens the website.
- **The old Jarvis data.** A one-time **Import** brings in the applications the previous version left in `data/` (the job board cache, Runway's Applied tab and the old tracker), merging duplicates. Imported applications without replies don't count toward the response rate or the screen rule, since replies weren't tracked for them.

Jobs live in the plan (`plan.jobs`), so they sync between the PC, the Mac and the phone like tasks do; two desktops reading the same inbox produce one record per application.
